About Me / À propos

I’m Frédéric Perron, a cybersecurity bachelor graduate from Polytechnique Montréal (2025) with a background in aviation security at Ottawa International Airport. I transitioned from physical/aviation security to cybersecurity to focus on protecting systems at scale—combining both my passions; security and technology/computers.

My experience covers network analysis, system hardening, threat modeling, vulnerability assessment, and both offensive and defensive security. I’ve worked with tools and environments across virtualized systems, cloud platforms, and databases, applying real-world attack scenarios to identify weaknesses and implement practical defenses.

I approach security from an adversarial mindset—understanding how systems break and designing them to be resilient. I’m also familiar with frameworks such as NIST CSF, MITRE ATT&CK, STRIDE, and COBIT, which I use to structure assessments and consulting work.

I’m particularly interested in helping startups and non-technical teams improve their security posture, prepare for audits, and build strong foundations before scaling. Refer to my "projects" and "my services" sections to learn more about my works and consulting services.

My Services / Mes Services

I help startups and small organizations identify security gaps, prepare for audits, and implement practical safeguards without hiring a full-time security team.

My focus is on cybersecurity readiness — ensuring the fundamentals are in place before pursuing frameworks like NIST CSF or SOC 2, depending on your preferences.

I typically work on:

  • Security readiness & hygiene reviews
  • Framework gap analysis (NIST CSF, SOC 2 readiness)
  • Security policies and incident response basics
  • Phishing risk reduction and awareness

My approach is simple: clear, structured, and aligned with real business risk — not unnecessary complexity. Making everything understandable from a business view to non-technical business owners is also one of my priorities.

If you need help understanding your current security posture or preparing for audit requirements, feel free to reach out by email or on LinkedIn, even if your question is simple and seems basic, that's what my services are for.
